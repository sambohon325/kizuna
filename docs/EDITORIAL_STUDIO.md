# Growth and Editorial Studio

The Editorial Studio turns one approved factual brief into a connected campaign: a Journal draft and platform-shaped copy for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

## Workflow

1. Open the marketing administrator and select **Editorial studio**.
2. Name the campaign and choose its content type.
3. Enter only verified facts that Kizuna is prepared to state publicly.
4. Describe the audience, communication goal, call to action, and optional target date.
5. Select **Prepare campaign**.
6. Review the blog and channel variants together.
7. Approve the facts and copy, then create a Journal draft.
8. Open Journal for final editing and deliberate publication.

## Mandatory review

Product announcements, pricing, discounts, partnerships, customer stories, incidents, outages, security, legal or policy statements, dates, availability, comparisons, guarantees, and financial claims require approval. Confidential, internal-only, NDA, secret, or unannounced material is blocked rather than rewritten.

Routine educational copy may be prepared as low risk when its factual brief has been approved. It still remains a draft in this slice.

## External channels

Social variants are currently reviewable and copyable. Kizuna does not yet authorize or post to external social accounts. That later connector layer must use revocable credentials, narrow scopes, visible schedules, per-channel delivery receipts, retry limits, and a global pause control.
