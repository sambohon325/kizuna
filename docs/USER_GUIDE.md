# Kizuna Studio user guide

Kizuna is a connected anime production studio. It helps creators develop an original story, direct visual and sound work, coordinate AI collaborators, manage production assets, and deliver a finished video without requiring every creator to operate every specialist control.

## Start here

1. Select **New production**.
2. Enter an original working title and logline.
3. Choose the production form: one-off, trailer, feature, ongoing series, or limited series.
4. Choose the destination, screen shape, target length, and release count.
5. Create the production and follow **Right now** for the next useful action.

Visiting a workspace never marks work complete. Milestones reflect saved production work and required compliance reviews.

## Choose your experience level

The **View** selector changes how much interface detail is visible. It never changes saved work, output quality, permissions, AI authority, approvals, or compliance.

- **Beginner** shows one primary task, the main creative canvas, and plain-language decisions.
- **Intermediate** reveals the complete craft sequence, supporting context, versions, and commonly adjusted controls.
- **Advanced** reveals specialist controls, technical metadata, infrastructure, and the densest professional workspace.

You can change levels at any time. Experience level and AI automation are separate choices.

## The five chapters

The top bar shows only the five chapters. Select **Imagine**, **Direct**, **Finish**, or **Studio** to open its tools in a dropdown; choosing a tool closes the menu. The bottom dock keeps the saved nine-stage production journey visible. Select any milestone to open that workspace; navigation never marks it complete. The contextual **Craft Compass** and **AI** buttons stay together on the right.

### Home

See productions, their current state, and the next real milestone. Change a production's release scope when its intended format, platform, orientation, length, or episode plan changes.

Home remembers the production you most recently opened. Its current-production card shows the release scope, factual milestone count, and next useful step. Select **Continue** to open that craft directly. Continue on another production card makes that production current without marking any unfinished work complete.

### Imagine

- **AI Crew:** choose which creative partners help, how they work, and what requires approval.
- **Writer's Room:** move from concept to structure, scenes, screenplay, and revision.
- **Style Lab:** define original Creative DNA and a respectful Anime Craft Compass.
- **Characters:** build visual character cards, story history, relationships, model sheets, and approved assets.
- **Worlds:** build reusable locations with story purpose, geography, staging, visual construction, layers, and light.

### Direct

- **Shots:** turn approved story beats into visual coverage and storyboard frames.
- **Timeline:** organize source clips, shape picture rhythm, create a review copy, and export the final video.
- **Audio:** build dialogue, music, sound effects, and ambience on a synchronized multitrack canvas.

### Finish

- **Composite:** assemble approved artwork as editable layers, then direct camera, grade, and motion.
- **Render:** see readiness, jobs, connected computers, schedules, throttles, and Hive capacity.

### Studio

- **Asset Library:** browse, approve, and reuse production assets. Intermediate adds metadata and versions.
- **Activity:** see what is working, waiting, finished, or needs attention. Intermediate adds technical history.
- **Help:** search tutorials, choose a learning path, or restart the studio tour.
- **Settings:** manage Workspace, AI & tools, Computers & costs, Storage & backups, Team & access, and Identity & rights. Advanced adds server Operations.

Under **Team & access**, choose one or more productions and assign Viewer, Editor, or Owner access. Kizuna emails the secure invitation when studio email is configured and always shows a copyable link as a fallback. Pending invitations show their expiry date. Select **Send again** to issue a fresh link and email, or **Renew link** after expiry; the older link stops working immediately. Viewer is the safest role for someone who only needs to explore or review a production.

## Use the Kizuna Assistant

Select the **AI** button at the right of the bottom production dock from any workspace. The Assistant receives the current workspace, selected production, visible context, and saved workflow state. The adjacent Compass icon opens craft guidance for the page you are currently using.

Useful requests include:

- “What should I do next?”
- “Explain the controls on this screen.”
- “Help me improve this scene without changing its intent.”
- “Check character and world continuity.”
- “Prepare a review checklist for this stage.”

The Assistant can recommend and route work, but it does not silently change production data.

## Build and direct the AI Crew

- **Review first** lets agents propose work and wait for approval.
- **Autopilot** delegates ready stages to the enabled crew.
- **Manual** pauses all agents until the creator enables them.
- **Customize crew** lets the creator choose departments, names, traits, instructions, routes, and independence.

Use Beginner to choose the working relationship. Move to Intermediate or Advanced when you need role-specific models, standing directions, or specialist controls.

When you choose **Customize crew** in Beginner:

1. Pick the creative craft you want help with.
2. Give that partner a name, useful personality traits, and a supervision level.
3. Tell it what it should always remember, then save it.

AI routes, model overrides, and detailed tool permissions remain available in Intermediate and Advanced without changing the partner you already created.

## Review and deliver

1. Open **Direct → Timeline**.
2. Open **Review & deliver**.
3. Create and watch a lightweight review copy.
4. Resolve story, continuity, sound, rights, and compliance findings.
5. Choose Full HD or Ultra HD and export the final video.
6. For long productions, expand **Use the Hive** to distribute the export.
7. Follow progress in **Studio → Activity**.

Trial accounts are limited to the configured trial duration and export allowance, and trial exports are watermarked.

## Originality, rights, and professional identity

Kizuna supports original productions. It does not support fan fiction based on known properties. Broad eras, traditions, techniques, archetypes, and art-direction traits can inform new work; a specific title, character, artist identity, recording, or protected visual language must not be copied.

Record ownership, licenses, voice consent, source notes, and professional authorization. Verified professionals can document prior work they created or are authorized to represent, but verification does not create a general compliance exemption.

Required compliance findings must be resolved before gated stages or publishing. A clear scan reduces risk; it is not a legal guarantee, and creators remain responsible for what they publish.

## Local, cloud, and Hive production

Kizuna can use its server, cloud providers, and connected Windows, macOS, and Linux computers together. The companion reports approved capabilities rather than personal files. Each computer can have schedules, allowed tasks, concurrency, CPU, GPU, memory, pause, and drain controls.

Large originals and renders can remain on user-controlled storage while Kizuna keeps the production graph, approvals, audit history, lightweight records, and previews connected.

## Getting unstuck

- Follow **Right now** for the next meaningful action.
- Select **Why this?** for a short explanation of why Kizuna recommends that action.
- Open **AI** from the bottom production dock for contextual help.
- Open **Studio → Help** to search the full manual.
- Open **Studio → Activity** when background work is delayed or failed.
- Move to Intermediate or Advanced only when the current task needs deeper controls.
