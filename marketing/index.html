QUESTIONS

Start with the story.

Is Kizuna an AI video generator?

Kizuna is broader than a generator. It is an AI-assisted storytelling and production platform that connects planning, creation, revisions, assets, approvals, compute, and delivery. Generation providers are tools within that process.

Do I need professional production experience?

No. Beginner view keeps the next useful action clear and lets AI partners prepare work for your review. Intermediate and Advanced views reveal deeper craft and production controls without forcing a separate project.

Can professionals keep full control?

Yes. AI roles can be configured or paused, work remains reviewable, and advanced users can open the underlying craft workspaces and production settings.

What is included in the free trial?

The trial lasts 7 days. Trial video exports are limited to 60 seconds and include a Kizuna watermark. Availability may depend on the current protected-signup rollout.

Is Anime Studio the entire Kizuna product?

No. Anime Studio is the first suite and proving ground for the shared Kizuna platform. Express, sequential storytelling, deeper motion, and other production experiences are planned to build on the same connected foundation.
